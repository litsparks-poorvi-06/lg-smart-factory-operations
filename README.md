🏭 LG Smart Factory
AI-Powered Operational Intelligence Platform

LG Smart Factory is an AI-assisted operational intelligence platform designed to unify production, warehouse, maintenance, quality, and safety operations into a single intelligent dashboard.

The platform combines real-time operational monitoring, AI-assisted analysis, predictive maintenance, incident management, computer vision, digital-twin simulation, and automated executive reporting to transform factory data into actionable insights.

⚙️ Core Capabilities
📊 Real-Time Factory Dashboard — KPIs, alerts, downtime, production and quality metrics
🤖 AI Factory Copilot — Gemini-powered analysis and cross-department decision support
🔧 Predictive Maintenance — risk scoring based on temperature, vibration, runtime and pressure
🚨 Incident Management — image-based incident reporting and severity classification
👁️ Vision Analysis — Gemini-powered analysis of uploaded incident images
🧠 Agentic Operations — specialized Production, Warehouse, Maintenance, Quality and Safety agents
🧬 Digital Twin Simulator — simulation of machine failures, demand surges, outages and other factory scenarios
📄 Executive Reports — automated KPI summaries and PDF reports
⚠️ Breakdown Alerts — detection of machines at breakdown risk with recommended actions
🏗️ Technology Stack

Python · Streamlit · Google Gemini · Supabase/PostgreSQL · Pandas · NumPy · Plotly · fpdf2

The platform uses Supabase for cloud data storage and role-based access, Streamlit for the interactive dashboard, and Google Gemini for AI-assisted analysis and vision capabilities.
